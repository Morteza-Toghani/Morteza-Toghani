# Morteza Toghani
### Artificial Intelligence Researcher  
Computational Intelligence • Representation Learning • Efficient Neural Systems

---

## Research Statement
I study intelligent systems through the principles of structure, representation, and computational efficiency. My work centers on designing neural architectures that move beyond brute-force scaling toward principled learning mechanisms grounded in mathematical reasoning and optimization theory.

I am particularly interested in how inductive bias, representation geometry, and optimization dynamics shape generalization in deep models, and how these factors can be leveraged to build systems that are both scalable and reliable.

---

## Core Research Directions
- Deep Neural Architectures and Optimization Theory  
- Representation Learning and Latent Structure Modeling  
- Computer Vision and High-Dimensional Perception Systems  
- Data-Efficient and Self-Supervised Learning  
- Efficient AI: Model Compression, Scaling Laws, and Computational Trade-offs

## Academic Vision
The next advances in artificial intelligence will not emerge from scale alone, but from structurally informed design, principled generalization, and computational responsibility.

My long-term objective is to contribute to the development of intelligent systems that are mathematically grounded, interpretable, robust under distributional shift, and efficient in their use of data and compute.
