### Research Focus

I work on **sample-efficient, uncertainty-aware reinforcement learning for real-world mobile manipulation** — closing the gap between simulation-trained policies and reliable deployment on physical robots operating under partial observability and contact-rich interaction.

My current line of work asks: *how can a manipulation policy explicitly represent what it doesn't know, and use that signal — not just reward — to explore and act more safely with fewer real-world samples?* This sits at the intersection of RL, uncertainty estimation, and embodied perception (vision, tactile, proprioception).

I'm applying this year for PhD / research internship positions in this exact direction, targeting labs doing real-world RL-based manipulation and locomanipulation research.
